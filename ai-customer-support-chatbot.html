Reduce repetitive support work without losing accuracy

A useful support chatbot should solve common questions quickly while staying honest about what it does not know. BizzBot is configured to answer from your business information and escalate when the request falls outside that scope.

The assistant can cover service details, order processes, policies, troubleshooting steps and contact information. It can also collect the context your staff need before a handover, reducing repeated questions on both sides.

For teams looking to reduce support ticket volume, this creates a practical first line of support while keeping complex, sensitive or exceptional cases with human staff.

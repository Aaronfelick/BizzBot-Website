Operations AI · Early access

Your team's questions don't stop at 6pm. Your supervisors do.

On every factory floor, warehouse and site, the same thing happens all day: someone hits a problem, and the only way forward is to phone a supervisor. BizzBot Operations AI puts your SOPs, manuals and escalation rules on WhatsApp — so staff get the answer themselves, in the language they actually speak.

The back-and-forth is the cost

A line operator hits an error code. A picker finds damaged stock. A night-shift technician isn't sure which procedure applies. None of these are hard questions — the answer is written down somewhere. But it's in a binder, or a manual in a language they don't read, or in one supervisor's head.

So they call. The supervisor stops what they're doing, or doesn't pick up, and the line waits. Multiply that by every shift and it becomes one of the quietest, largest costs in the business — paid in downtime, in interrupted managers, and in decisions made by guessing.

Operations AI answers those questions directly, from documents you approve, and gets a human involved only when a human is genuinely needed.

Lives on WhatsApp

No app to install, no login, no training session. It's the app already open on every phone on the floor, working on cheap handsets and patchy signal.

Speaks their language

Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Bengali, Tagalog, Arabic and English — including mixed-language messages and voice notes, which is how people actually write.

A photo can be the question

Send a picture of an error display, a damaged pallet, a label or a form. Describing a problem in a second language is the hardest part — this removes it.

Grounded in your documents

Answers come from your SOPs, manuals and work instructions. Not from the open internet, and not invented. What you approve is what it says.

Escalates instead of guessing

The most important thing it does is know when to stop. Anything outside its knowledge, or anything safety-related, routes to the right supervisor with the full context attached.

Every exchange logged

A searchable record of what was asked and what was answered — useful for audits, for training, and for spotting the same problem recurring across shifts.

Built for how Gulf workforces actually operate

A UAE production floor might run in five languages at once. Most workplace software quietly assumes everyone reads English, which is why so much of it goes unused and the phone call survives.

Operations AI is designed the other way round: the worker writes or speaks however is natural to them, and the system does the translating. Supervisors and managers see everything in English.

We start narrow on purpose — one department and one category of question, such as machine fault triage or shift handover — prove it works on real shifts, then widen. That is a deliberately smaller first step than most vendors will offer you, and it is the reason these projects succeed.

Where it fits

Food manufacturing and cold chain, warehousing and logistics, facilities management, construction and hospitality — any operation running shifts, with a multilingual frontline and procedures that matter.

It works alongside your customer-facing BizzBot chatbot, or entirely on its own.

Operations AI · Early access

Your bottleneck isn't like anyone else's

Every business has work that quietly eats time — the same questions asked over and over, the request that sits waiting for someone to route it, the information that lives in one person's head. Operations AI is custom AI automation built around your version of that problem. Not a product you configure. A system we design around how your business actually runs.

Tell us your bottleneck →View pricing

We start with your problem, not our product

Most automation tools hand you a platform and leave you to work out what to do with it. That is backwards, and it is why so much of it goes unused.

We start the other way round. We sit with how your business runs, find the work that is costing you most in time and interruptions, and build automation specifically around it. What gets built, what channel it runs on, what language it speaks and what it connects to are all decided by your operation — not by what happens to be in our template.

That means no two BizzBot Operations AI builds look the same, and it means we tell you honestly when a problem is not worth automating yet.

Any workflow

Answering repeated internal questions, routing and triaging requests, chasing approvals, logging and reporting, processing forms and documents, checking data across systems, escalating what needs a person.

Any channel

WhatsApp, your website, Microsoft Teams, Slack or email. It goes where your people already are, so nobody has to be persuaded to adopt anything.

Any input

Typed messages, photos, PDFs, scanned forms and spreadsheets. People describe problems however is natural to them — including just sending a picture.

Any language

English and Arabic, plus Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam and Tagalog — including mixed-language messages. Your team writes naturally; management reads it in English.

Grounded in your own data

Answers come from your documents, systems and rules. Not the open internet, and not invented. What you approve is what it says.

Knows when to stop

The most important thing it does is recognise its own limits. Anything outside its knowledge, or anything with real consequences, goes to the right person with full context attached.

Five businesses, five completely different builds

These are illustrations of the range, not a menu. Yours will look like none of them.

Food manufacturing

Frontline staff hit a machine fault or a process question and currently phone a supervisor. Instead they message in Hindi or Urdu, send a photo of the error display, and get the answer straight from the approved SOP — with anything safety-related routed to a human immediately.

Warehousing & logistics

Drivers and pickers check stock positions, delivery windows and exception procedures from their phone mid-shift, instead of radioing a coordinator who is already handling three other calls.

Facilities & property

A tenant or technician photographs a fault. It is classified, given a priority, matched to the right contractor and logged as a job — without a coordinator retyping any of it.

Clinics & professional services

Intake documents arrive as photos and PDFs, get read and checked for what is missing, and reach the right person already sorted — instead of an admin working through an inbox.

Multi-branch retail

Branch staff ask about pricing rules, returns policy or stock in another location and get one consistent answer, rather than five branches each interpreting the manual differently.

And whatever yours is

If your team keeps interrupting someone for the same information, or work keeps waiting on a person to move it along, that is the shape of problem this solves. The build follows from there.

How a build actually goes

We map it. We look at where time actually goes — what people ask, who they ask, how often, and what it costs when the answer is slow or wrong.

We scope one workflow. Not everything at once. One department, one category of work, chosen because it is the most painful and the most measurable.

We build and run it on real work. Grounded in your documents and systems, on the channel your people already use, with escalation built in from the start rather than added later.

Then we widen. Once it is proven on live operations, we extend it to the next workflow. Deliberately smaller first steps than most vendors will offer you — and the reason these projects succeed instead of stalling.

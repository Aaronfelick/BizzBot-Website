Real estate lead generation

Turn property enquiries into qualified leads

A real estate chatbot can answer listing questions, collect buyer preferences, qualify budgets and pass organised enquiries to your sales team at any hour.

Get your chatbot →View pricing

Built for the way property buyers actually ask questions

Prospects rarely follow a perfect form. They ask about price, location, payment plans, availability, handover dates and viewing times in different orders. A context-aware chatbot keeps the conversation natural while collecting the details your agents need.

BizzBot can be trained on approved project information, listing data, FAQs and lead-routing rules. When a question requires a human, the bot can capture the customer’s contact details and prepare a concise handover.

This gives agencies and developers an automated lead capture bot without forcing customers through a long form before they receive useful information.

Listing answers

Viewing requests

Collect preferred dates and route high-intent prospects to the correct agent.

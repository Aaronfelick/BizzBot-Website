Chatbot vs live chat ROI

The strongest support model often combines automation and people

Compare chatbot vs live chat ROI, response coverage, support workload and customer experience before choosing an automation strategy.

What to consider

Live chat gives customers direct access to a person, but coverage depends on staffing, working hours and queue volume. An AI chatbot can answer frequent questions instantly and remain available outside office hours.

ROI should not be measured only by replacing staff. Useful measures include faster first response, fewer repetitive tickets, better lead capture, more complete handovers and the number of conversations resolved without escalation.

A practical model lets the chatbot handle approved, repeatable questions and sends complex or sensitive cases to a human. This protects quality while reducing avoidable workload.

Before deployment, identify the questions that consume the most time, the answers that can be standardised and the situations that must always reach a person. Those boundaries determine whether the automation will create real value.

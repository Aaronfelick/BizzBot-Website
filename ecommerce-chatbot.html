E-commerce chatbot

Help shoppers choose, buy and get support faster

An AI sales assistant for the questions between search and checkout

Customers often need clarity on sizing, compatibility, delivery, returns or product differences before they buy. A trained e-commerce chatbot can answer those questions immediately using your approved catalogue and store policies.

The assistant can guide product discovery, surface relevant choices, collect contact details for unavailable items and reduce repetitive support tickets after purchase.

BizzBot supports website chat widget integration for Shopify, Wix, WordPress and custom storefronts. The experience is tailored to your brand rather than presented as a generic third-party flow.
